# Catalogue content review

This catalogue belongs to the three-catalogue Maxiview project.

- Retain existing specifications when their source support is uncertain.
- Retain source-status notes in the expandable optometrist references under (i). Do not render disclaimer or source-confirmation badges on customer-facing catalogue, comparison or summary pages (Kelvin, 21 November 2026).
- Do not remove existing content solely because a reviewer cannot verify it.
- Preserve pricing, replacement terms, complimentary controls and printing behavior during content updates.

- Use unnumbered mechanism cards; do not add decorative 01/02/03 labels. Keep product counts, study numbers, prices, follow-up visit identities and guarantee terms.
- Keep the persistent summary controls in a dedicated page-footer region outside the scrolling catalogue so they cannot cover content. Align corresponding card rows by their content, without fixed spacer offsets.
