D.I.M.S. with Triple Enhanced Design

Three design updates in MiYOSMART iQ.

Closer to the centre

Treatment segments start closer to the lens centre, aiming the treatment signal at the nearby peripheral retina.

Higher defocus power

+4.5 D, compared with +3.5 D in MiYOSMART, creates a stronger myopic-defocus treatment signal.

Wider treatment coverage

A 41 mm zone, compared with 33 mm in MiYOSMART, extends treatment coverage and supports larger frames.

Everyday vision and appearance

MiYOSMART iQ also uses an optimized FreeForm back surface, designed to reduce optical distortions and give a flatter lens profile.

A coating made for daily handling

S.P.A.R.K. provides improved scratch resistance and lasting easy-clean performance compared with the previous MiYOSMART generation.
