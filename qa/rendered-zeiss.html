Why short-sightedness can keep progressing

Children’s days have changed.

Illustrative images. Not trial participants.

More time indoors, more near work

Children now spend more time on screens and close work. Their eyes see less varied, more repetitive visual signals through the day, which may contribute to myopia progression.

Visual signals influence eye growth

Research suggests some signals may stimulate the eye to grow longer, while others, such as myopic defocus, may help slow it. MyoActive is designed around this idea.

ZEISS M.O.V.E. technology

Clear vision. Changing signals.

Clear vision at the centre

A 7 mm clear central zone corrects distance vision for everyday activities.

Different powers. A deliberately non-repeating pattern.

Tiny treatment elements use different positive powers in a non-repeating arrangement. As your child looks around, this creates changing myopia-control signals. The design aims to reduce repetition of the signal reaching the eye.

ClearFocus back-surface design

An optimized back surface supports the prescription correction and intended treatment signal as your child looks through different parts of the lens.
