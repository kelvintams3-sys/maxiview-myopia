Lens design, safety and materials

How one lenslet works

Safety

In the original two-year trial, no severe adverse events were reported; two mild events were judged unrelated to the lens. The Stellest 2.0 one-year study reported no device-related adverse events. This does not mean any lens is risk-free.

Who the studies covered

Original Stellest trial: ages 8–13. Stellest 2.0 crossover study: ages 6–10. The U.S. practitioner instructions list ages 6–12 and −0.75 D to −4.50 D; these are U.S. indications and separate from local availability.
